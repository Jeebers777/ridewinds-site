You train for it. You fuel for it. Now pick the wind for it.

RideWinds names the day and hour the wind is behind you, on your segments and your routes. After the ride, it tells you how much the wind really helped or hurt.

Before a hard effort

The hour your PR is on

Nobody going for a record leaves training or nutrition to chance. So why let the wind be an oversight? A tailwind is worth seconds on a segment, and a headwind can take more than that away.

RideWinds ranks the segments on your rides by the wind that's forecast, names the hour, and says what that wind is worth: "Tuesday 3 pm, +8 km/h tailwind, 5 s under your PR." It allows for the shelter on that road, from your own rides.

Segments need a Strava connection. Predicted times are estimates.

After the ride

Was it the wind, or was it you?

Every ride gets a debrief: what was forecast, what blew over the district, and what reached you on the road. "The wind cost you about 7 minutes."

So a hard, slow day is no longer a mystery, and on a fast one you know how much was you. Share the file from your head unit, or connect Strava and the app finds the ride.

Every other day

Which ride, which way, and when

Not chasing anything today? Best ride picks which of your routes to do, which way round, and when to leave, so you decide where the wind helps and where it hurts.

The map colours every stretch for the hours you will be on it.

How to join

  1. On your iPhone, install Apple's free TestFlight app.
  2. Tap this link and choose Accept, then Install.
  3. Open RideWinds, say where you ride, and add half a dozen of your favourite local rides: from Strava, or a GPX or FIT file. Connect Strava for your segments.

Any feedback helps, even "I didn't get it". Take a screenshot in the app and choose Share Beta Feedback, or write to hello@ridewinds.app.
