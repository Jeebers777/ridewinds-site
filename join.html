Which ride, which way, and when

RideWinds scores your own routes by the wind forecast along the road. After the ride it tells you whether it was the wind or you.

Before you go

Best ride picks from your routes

Tell it how far you want to go and what you want from the wind, such as a tailwind home. It ranks your routes, in both directions, and names the hour to leave.

It also says when not to go. The pick shown here is a wet day: rain is a veto, not a small penalty.

On the map

The wind along the actual road

Every stretch is coloured for the hour you will be on it: red for a headwind, amber for a crosswind, green for a tailwind.

Drag the time to see how a later start changes the ride, or play it through.

After the ride

Was it the wind or you?

Share the file from your head unit, or connect Strava and the app finds the ride. The debrief compares what was forecast, what blew over the district and what reached you on the road.

Then it puts a figure on it: what the wind cost you in minutes, stretch by stretch. These are estimates, and the app says "about" wherever it is estimating.

How to join

  1. On your iPhone, install Apple's free TestFlight app.
  2. Tap this link and choose Accept, then Install.
  3. Open RideWinds, say where you ride, and add a route or two: from Strava, or a GPX or FIT file.

Any feedback helps, even "I didn't get it". Shake the phone in the app, or write to hello@ridewinds.app.
