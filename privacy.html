RideWinds privacy

Last updated 24 October 2026

RideWinds is a weather app for cyclists made by an individual developer in Canberra, Australia. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. This page says exactly what leaves your phone and why.

Your location

If you allow it, RideWinds uses your location on the phone to show where you are on the map, to set your weather place, and to let a ride start from where you are. Your location is not sent to us — we have no server — and is not shared with anyone. You can decline location access and search for a town instead.

Weather forecasts

To fetch forecasts, the app sends the coordinates of your weather place and of points along your saved routes to Open-Meteo, the forecast provider. No name, account or device identifier goes with them. Open-Meteo's own privacy policy applies to that request.

Your rides

Routes you import (GPX files, Strava routes, Quick out-and-back) are stored only on your phone and in your iCloud device backup. They are never uploaded to us.

Strava (optional)

If you connect Strava, RideWinds asks for read-only access to your routes and activities so you can import them. The sign-in is handled by Strava; your Strava password is never seen by the app. Access tokens are stored in the iPhone Keychain. RideWinds never posts to Strava and never reads anything other than the rides and routes you choose to import. Disconnecting in Settings revokes the access on Strava's side as well.

Tour de Cure

The Tour de Cure tab shows content (story, dedications, fundraising progress) fetched from this website. Donations happen on Tour de Cure's own site, under their privacy policy; the app does not see them.

Apple

Purchase of the app, TestFlight beta participation and crash reports (if you have opted in on your device) are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.
